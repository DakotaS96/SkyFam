# Why I Built SkyFam

SkyFam began after my mom passed away. I wanted a simple way to stay connected with my dad, share messages and photos with him, and help keep him informed and safe during severe weather—all through an always-on screen that did not require him to navigate several apps or constantly check his phone.

I work in IT and enjoy tinkering with computers, Raspberry Pis, self-hosted services, and older hardware. Although SkyFam can be technical behind the scenes, I wanted the family experience to be simple: turn on the display and everything is already there.

FamilyChat provided a straightforward way to send messages, photos, and videos directly to my dad’s screen. The project gradually grew to include a photo slideshow, internet radio, browser games, livestream support, and tools for controlling what appears on connected displays.

Severe-weather awareness also became an important part of SkyFam. As a longtime viewer of Ryan Hall’s YouTube broadcasts, I wanted my dad to have an easy way to find that coverage when it matters. SkyFam can display YallBot and provide access to Ryan Hall’s live severe-weather coverage through its embedded YouTube player without requiring someone to search for the broadcast.

SkyFam is self-hosted so that family messages, photos, videos, passwords, and other personal information remain under the administrator’s control. What began as something made for my own family eventually became a project that I thought could help other families stay connected, informed, entertained, and included.

SkyFam is an independent project built from a real family need. I am sharing it so others can adapt it for their own families and perhaps make staying connected a little easier.

SkyFam is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ryan Hall, YouTube, or any third-party service or project it displays or integrates with.

## Support SkyFam

SkyFam is free and open-source, and I intend to keep it that way.

If you enjoy the project and would like to support its continued development, you can make an optional contribution—but please do not feel obligated. Simply using or sharing SkyFam is support enough.

I work on SkyFam in my spare time, so I cannot promise individual technical support or specific timelines. Bug reports and suggestions are welcome through GitHub Issues, and I will review them when I can.

If you choose to contribute, think of it less as buying me a coffee and more as buying me an energy drink for the next round of development.
